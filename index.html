About Me

Hi 👋, I am Yixuan Zhang, a master's student in Data Science and Information Technology at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), Tsinghua University. I am supervised by Prof. Wenbo Ding in the Smart Sensing and Robotics (SSR) Lab.

I earned a Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) degree in Computer Science and Technology from Northeastern University (NEU), where I was supervised by Prof. Changzeng Fu in the Human-centered Affective Computing and Interaction (HACI) Lab.

My research interests include Embodied Intelligence and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI).
